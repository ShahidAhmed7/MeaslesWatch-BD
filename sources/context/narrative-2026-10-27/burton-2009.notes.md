Read the complete English article, including key data sources, review processes,
estimation heuristics and discussion. Administrative ratios can be biased by
incomplete reporting, doses outside a target group and inaccurate population
estimates. Reviewed estimates combine national reports, survey findings and local
knowledge. The authors discuss uncertainty and gaps in empirical validation.

Use: foundational methodological context, not a description of every current
WUENIC procedure. Do not adopt the paper's old vaccine schedule or uncertainty
ranges as 2026 guidance. The case study uses current WHO definitions separately.
