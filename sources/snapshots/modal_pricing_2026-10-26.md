Modal is serverless, which means that we instantly autoscale up and
down for you based on request volume. For spiky or unpredictable
workloads, we are more cost-effective than fixed on-demand/reserved
compute.

## Frequently asked questions

- ### What counts as billable time?








Modal bills you during application load time and during the time your container is processing inputs. By default, we keep containers alive for 60 seconds after the last inputs before we shut them down, which we also bill for. However, this timeout length is configurable. Once your application has scaled down to zero containers you no longer get charged.

- ### How are CPU and memory usage metered?








Modal continuously records CPU and memory consumption. CPU usage is tracked in terms of fractional physical cores, allowing for higher utilization compared to providers that set minimum thresholds for core charges. Note that each physical core is equivalent to 2 vCPUs as offered by AWS or GCP.



You'll be charged based on whichever is higher: the amount you [request](https://modal.com/docs/guide/resources#configuring-cpu-and-memory) or the amount you actually use. Each container has a minimum request of 128 MiB of memory and 0.125 CPU cores, which you can increase if you need more resources.

- ### Why are Sandboxes and Functions billed at different CPU and memory rates?








Modal Sandboxes are priced at the rate of [non-preemptible Functions](https://modal.com/docs/guide/preemption#non-preemptible-functions).



Modal Functions are subject to preemption by default; while interruptions are rare, they can happen. CPU Sandboxes, on the other hand, are not subject to preemption. On Modal, a 3x multiplier is applied to the CPU and Memory list price for non-preemptible resources.

- ### How does serverless pricing differ from traditional on-demand pricing?








Modal's serverless pricing can't be directly compared to traditional on-demand or spot instance pricing.



Traditional cloud compute doesn't have instant spin-up times. You might spend 10+ minutes provisioning an instance and another 5 terminating it. You also have to commit to a minimum usage time per instance, usually between 1 minute and 1 hour. All this time is billable. In contrast, _Modal spins containers_ up and down in 1-2 seconds. You don't commit to any minimum usage time per instance.



Even though our per-hour prices may sometimes appear higher, your overall costs are lower if your request volumes are spiky or unpredictable. Modal autoscales fast, so you pay for significantly less non-execution time versus traditional compute. You also save on cost by not having to over-provision compute to handle peak loads.

- ### What's an example of real-world CPU billing?








You schedule a job to run once a day for an hour. The job uses 50% of one CPU core and 256 MiB of memory.



This job will cost $0.02365/day for the CPU, and $0.00200/day for the memory, adding up to _$0.02565/day_, or _$0.77 over 30 days_.

- ### What's an example of real-world GPU billing?








You run stable diffusion on an A10G GPU. This will run for about 1.5 seconds to generate each image, while using 4 GiB RAM and 1 CPU.



This will cost approximately $0.000458/image in GPU charges, $0.000020/image in CPU charges, and $0.000013/image in memory charges, adding up to _$0.000491/image_ in total charges, i.e., _$0.491 per 1,000 images_.

- ### Do I need to purchase credits?








Workspaces receive a free allotment of compute per month, but usage on the Starter and Team plans is pay-as-you-go and does not require pre-purchasing compute or tokens. If you want to limit your monthly spend, you can [set a Workspace budget](https://modal.com/docs/guide/budgets#workspace-budgets).

- ### Can I add more than three team members on the starter plan?








The starter plan allows up to three users in a workspace. Upgrading to the team plan unlocks an unlimited number of seats.

- ### What kinds of applications can I deploy using Modal?








You can deploy any application that can be containerized, using Python. Modal is especially well-suited for compute-intensive workloads that benefit from horizontal scaling. This includes ML inference at scale, model training & fine-tuning, data pipelines, numerical & scientific computing, compute-heavy job queues, and much more.

- ### Can I use my AWS, GCP, or Azure credits on Modal?








At the moment, credits from AWS, GCP, or Azure cannot be applied towards your Modal usage. However, we have a special partnership with AWS that allows eligible startups to unlock additional Modal credits if they are part of AWS Activate. To find out if your company is eligible, please [apply](https://modal.com/startups) to our startup credits program.

- ### What does my included usage cover?








Included usage applies to compute: Functions and Sandboxes, billed by the second for CPU, memory, and GPU time. Volumes include up to 1 TiB free.



It does not apply to Shared Endpoints, which are billed per token rather than per second. Token usage is always billed, starting from your first request. To cap out-of-pocket charges, see [spend limits](https://modal.com/docs/guide/budgets#spend-limits).

- ### I'm on the Starter plan. Why am I being charged?








Starter has no monthly fee and includes $30 of compute (GPUs, CPUs, Memory) per month. You're billed when you exceed that, or when you use something the included compute doesn't cover.



Free usage of Shared Endpoints is not part of the Starter plan. Tokens are billed from your first request, so they will appear on your invoice even if you haven't used your full $30 of included compute. This took effect September 1, 2026.
