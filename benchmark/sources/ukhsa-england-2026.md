## England measles epidemiology 2026

This report describes measles epidemiology in England from 1 January 2026.

## Situational update

In 2024 there were 2,911 laboratory-confirmed measles cases in England, the highest number of cases recorded annually, since 2012. In 2025, there were a total of 958 laboratory-confirmed measles cases. Data for [2023 to 2026](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/measles-epidemiology-2023) and [historical data](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/measles-confirmed-cases/confirmed-cases-of-measles-in-england-and-wales-by-region-and-age-2012-to-2014) are available.

Between 1 January 2026 and 12 October 2026, there have been 1,030 laboratory-confirmed measles cases reported in England. An additional 27 cases were reported since the last publication on 17 September 2026 (Figures 1 and 2). In 2026, measles activity started to increase in February, peaked in May and has declined and stabilised since then.

## Summary

The number of laboratory-confirmed measles cases by month of symptom onset in 2026 to date are:

- January: 105
- February: 142
- March: 144
- April: 156
- May: 215
- June: 154
- July: 52
- August: 33
- September: 26
- October: 3 (to date)

The [UKHSA data dashboard](https://ukhsa-dashboard.data.gov.uk/vaccine-preventable-diseases/measles) provides tabular data on the total number of cases by week of onset in 2026.

The majority (629 out of 1,030, 61%) of these cases were in children aged 10 years and under, and 33% (339 out of 1,030) were in young people and adults aged 15 years and over (Table 1). 50% (520 out of 1,030) of these cases have been in London, 16% (162 out of 1,030) in the West Midlands, and 11% (111 out of 1,030) in the North West. However, all regions have reported at least one confirmed case with symptom onset since January 2026. A total of 98 upper tier local authorities (UTLAs) have reported at least one confirmed case with symptom onset since January 2026, with the highest numbers reported in Enfield (111 out of 1,030, 11%), Birmingham (82 out of 1,030, 8%) and Hertfordshire (64 out of 1,030, 6%) (Table 2a).

The vaccination status of all confirmed cases is published quarterly in the related [health protection reports](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/measles-mumps-and-rubella-lab-confirmed-cases-in-england-2026).

Three measles related deaths have been reported to date in 2026: one was an acute measles death in a child, another was a death in a child due to late effects of measles, and the third was a death in an adult with an underlying immunological problem. Information on historical measles-related deaths is available on the [notifications and deaths webpage](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/measles-deaths-by-age-group-from-1980-to-2013-ons-data/measles-notifications-and-deaths-in-england-and-wales-1940-to-2013).

## Recent activity

There have been 19 laboratory -confirmed measles cases with symptom onset date in the 4 weeks since 14 September 2026. Data reporting lags have the greatest impact on the most recent 4 weeks and therefore the reported figures are likely to underestimate activity. London accounted for the highest proportion of these cases (58%, 11 out of 19). A total of 16% (3 out of 19) of cases have been in the North West, and 11% (2 out of 19) in the North East. A total of 12 UTLAs have reported at least one confirmed case with symptom onset since 14 September 2026.
