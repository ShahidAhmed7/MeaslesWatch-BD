# Approved public health analysis specification

Approved scope: user requested implementation of the phased portfolio redesign,
27 October 2026, Asia/Dhaka. Settings: `.devin/portfolio.toml`.

This is exploratory reanalysis after inspecting the data, not a new preregistered or
unseen confirmatory experiment. The original statistical and LLM studies remain intact.

1. Compare January–May all-age laboratory-confirmed reports in 2026 with the same
   months in every complete 2012–2025 year. Report the historical maximum and median;
   retain all years and repeat through March/April regardless of which looks strongest.
2. Explain source-specific first/second-dose coverage changes in 2024–2025. Independently
   reconcile doses and target population against WHO records, then reproduce the
   source author's log accounting identity. Shares of log change are arithmetic,
   not causal explanations of the outbreak or counts of missed children.
3. Repeat the magnitude comparison with total classified counts. Explain classification
   composition without adding suspected cases or interpreting laboratory share as
   positivity. Do not infer changes in testing practice from composition alone.

The August snapshot stays frozen. Current source checks have their own retrieval
date and response checksums; revised values cannot silently replace original values.
All 2026 counts are provisional; June–July are descriptive, lag-sensitive extensions.
No monthly age breakdown, population infection rate, causal effect, susceptibility
estimate, clinical use or prospective warning performance is inferred.

Each headline needs a claim record including period, population, source, formula,
unit, verification and permitted interpretation. Source mismatches qualify or remove
headlines. New artifacts go to a new directory and are not overwritten. The default
dashboard and short brief must explain the evidence without technical prerequisites.
