# Junior Data Scientist (Fixed-Term)

Posted on 15th October 2026

[Apply](mailto:hr@chrfbd.org?subject=Application%20for%20JUNIOR%20DATA%20SCIENTIST)

## Junior Data Scientist

#### Job Description

**Apply by 29 October 2026**

**Use your data science skills to make a difference in child health.**

Are you curious about data, passionate about solving problems, and eager to apply your skills to real scientific challenges?

At the Child Health Research Foundation (CHRF), we use data to understand infectious diseases, track emerging health threats, and generate evidence that can improve child health in Bangladesh and beyond.

We are looking for a motivated Junior Data Scientist to join our interdisciplinary research team. This is an opportunity for an early career professional to develop technical expertise while contributing to research with real public health impact.

**Why join CHRF -**

1\. Meaningful work: Apply data science to questions that can influence health research, clinical practice, and public health policy.

2\. Interdisciplinary science: Collaborate with researchers working in epidemiology, microbiology, genomics, and infectious diseases.

3\. Professional growth: Develop your analytical expertise through exposure to diverse research questions and complex datasets.

4\. Scientific contribution: Be part of a Bangladesh based organization generating evidence of national and international relevance.

**Location:** Dhaka, Bangladesh

**How to apply -**

Please email **hr@chrfbd.org** by **29 October 2026** with **your CV (maximum 2 pages) and a brief cover letter (maximum 1 page)** explaining why you want to join CHRF and what skills or experience you would bring to the role. You are welcome to highlight relevant academic, personal, or professional data science projects. Please use **“Junior Data Scientist”** in the email subject line.

Only shortlisted candidates will be contacted.

_CHRF is an equal opportunity employer. We welcome qualified applicants regardless of race, color, religion, sex, gender, or disability status_

#### Required Candidate Profile

**Who we are looking for -**

**Essential qualifications and skills**

1\. A bachelor’s degree in Statistics, Mathematics, Computer Science, Data Science, Engineering, Bioinformatics, or a related quantitative discipline.

2\. A good foundation in statistics, analytical reasoning, and problem solving.

3\. Working knowledge of at least one analytical programming language, such as Python, R, or Stata.

4\. Interest in scientific research and a willingness to learn new methods and tools.

5\. Ability to communicate findings clearly in English and work collaboratively.

**Additional skills that would be an advantage**

1\. Experience with statistical analysis, machine learning, or data visualization.

2\. Familiarity with SQL, data management, or reproducible programming workflows.

3\. Experience with research projects, independent analytical projects, or open source contributions.

_You do not need previous experience in public health research to apply. We welcome candidates with strong quantitative skills and an interest in using them to address health challenges._

#### Salary

Salary: Negotiable based on qualifications and experience Benefits: Contributory provident fund, festival allowances (Eid, Puja, Christmas, and Baishakhi), and a two day weekend.

#### Role

**What you will do -**

1\. Clean, organize, validate, and analyze complex datasets generated through clinical studies, laboratories, and public health surveillance.

2\. Apply statistical methods to identify patterns, investigate research questions, and generate meaningful insights.

3\. Explore opportunities to use machine learning, statistical modelling, and data visualization to support scientific research.

4\. Develop reproducible analytical workflows and contribute to the quality and reliability of research data.

5\. Communicate analytical findings through clear visualizations, reports, and presentations.

6\. Collaborate across disciplines and contribute ideas to improve how we collect, manage, and analyze data.

7\. Support CHRF’s Building Scientists for Bangladesh initiative and contribute to developing the next generation of researchers.
