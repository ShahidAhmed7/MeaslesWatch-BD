# Work after the frozen research MVP

The implementation and measured study are complete, but the current verifier is
experimental. Its limitations are material. These priorities do not retrospectively
change the recorded experiment or turn its existing questions into fresh test data.

1. **Correct scope contracts in a new adapter/verifier version.** Interpretation
   booleans and dates often inherit `all_ages` instead of `not_applicable`; several
   non-Texas count slots lose their denominator population. Define explicit typed
   scopes, use independent fixtures, and retain current v1 outputs and source snapshots.
2. **Preserve complete arithmetic provenance.** Record every operand and support
   alternative genuine total rows. A citation to one age component cannot establish
   a sum of three age groups. Check entailment beyond valid source/line existence.
3. **Retain specific scientific limitations in rendered answers.** Keep explicit
   provisional/conflicting-window explanations; flag missing fields and distinguish
   narrative cautions from canonical category strings. Do not certify arbitrary prose.
4. **Evaluate revisions on newly frozen independent questions.** The current
   external-v1 questions have been inspected and are now development evidence for
   future changes. A replay against them is post hoc regression checking, not a new
   unbiased accuracy estimate. Add genuinely external human review if available.
5. **Broaden statistical validation when data support it.** Study temporal residual
   dependence and parameter uncertainty while retaining simple comparators. Obtain
   release vintages and independently dated event labels before claims about real-time
   availability or operational outbreak detection. More complex models may not help.
6. **Operational hardening follows scientific correction.** Test disconnections and
   recovery deliberately, add fresh seeds/publications if budget permits, and keep
   original failure-inclusive results. Reservations are not provider billing caps.

Numeric accuracy alone is insufficient for release as a trusted analyst. Any revised
reliability claim must be supported by its own saved, independently evaluated results.
